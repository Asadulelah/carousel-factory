02
why HTML

design systems beat design moods.

Canva asks how you feel today. A stylesheet doesn't care.

Every slide inherits one theme block: change a single variable and the entire deck restyles. Consistency stops being discipline and becomes physics.

03
the machinery

the whole factory is ~130 lines of Python.

One headless browser. It finds every .slide, measures it, and clips a pixel-perfect screenshot at exactly the size Instagram wants.

No design tool subscription. No export dialog. No hands.
